Privacy Policy for DhikrDaily

Effective date: 15 September 2026 · Last updated: 20 November 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Bytesolix ("we", "us" or "our") handles information when you use the DhikrDaily mobile application (the "App"), a tasbeeh and Darood counter with auto-count and reminders. By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

No personal data

DhikrDaily is local-first. We do not collect, store or share any personal information. There is no account or login, no cloud sync, no analytics or crash-reporting service, and no advertising in the App.

Data stored only on your device

The following is created by you and stays on your device. We never receive it:

  • Your counters (tasbeeh and dhikr names, targets and counts)
  • Daily statistics and streaks
  • Appearance settings and reminder choices
  • Voice recordings you choose to make of your own recitation

Uninstalling the App deletes all of this data from your device.

Voice recordings (microphone)

The App asks for microphone access only if you choose to record your own voice reciting a dhikr. The recording is saved as an audio file inside the App's private storage on your device, is played back only within the App, and is never uploaded. You can delete it in the App, and you can deny or revoke microphone access at any time in your device settings without affecting the rest of the App.

Backups you control

Export creates a JSON backup file of your counters and settings and opens your device's share sheet. Where that file goes (for example, your files app, cloud drive or a messaging app) is entirely your choice, and is then governed by that service's privacy policy. Import reads a backup file you pick. We never receive your backups.

Dua library (internet connection)

When you browse the online dua library, the App downloads dua content from UmmahAPI. These requests contain only the category being requested; no personal data, counters or identifiers are sent. As with any internet request, the provider's servers can see your device's IP address and basic technical request information. All other features work without an internet connection.

2. Permissions

  • Notifications – to show the reminders you set and the auto-count notification. You can turn reminders off in Settings at any time.
  • Microphone – only to record your own recitation, as described above.
  • Background audio and foreground service (Android) – so auto-count, recitation and count sounds can keep running while the screen is off. A persistent notification is shown while this is active.
  • Media buttons – Bluetooth or car hands-free play/pause can pause and resume an active auto-count session. No data from connected devices is collected.

3. How We Use Information

Because we do not collect personal information, we do not use it for any purpose. Data stored on your device is used only by the App to provide its features. We do not sell, rent or share any personal information.

4. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party service:

  • UmmahAPI – provides dua library content (see above)

The App contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

We do not hold any of your data, so there is nothing for us to retain or delete. All App data lives on your device; you can remove it at any time by deleting counters and recordings in the App, clearing the App's data in your device settings, or uninstalling the App. Backup files you exported remain wherever you saved them until you delete them.

6. Children's Privacy

The App can be used by people of all ages. It does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

7. Security

Your data is kept in the App's private storage on your device, protected by your device's own security. Dua library requests are made over encrypted connections (HTTPS). No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, so we also recommend keeping your device locked and your backups in a safe place.

8. Your Rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR in the EU/UK or the CCPA in California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or export personal information. Because we do not collect or hold personal information, you exercise full control over your data directly in the App, including exporting it with the backup feature. If you have any questions, contact us using the details below.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes are posted on this page and in the App with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.
