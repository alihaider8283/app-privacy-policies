PDFluent Privacy Policy

PDFluent: Translate & Learn · Last updated: 21 November 2026

In short

  • You can use PDFluent without an account. Signing in with Google is optional and only needed to redeem an ad-free pass.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may use your device's advertising ID to show and measure ads.
  • Your PDFs, saved words, highlights and progress stay on your device.
  • We do not sell your data.

1. About this policy

This policy explains how the PDFluent Android app ("PDFluent", "the app", "we") handles your information. PDFluent helps you learn English by reading PDF documents and translating words into your language.

2. Information stored on your device

The following data is created by you and stored only in the app's private storage on your device. We do not have access to it.

  • PDF files you import: a copy is kept inside the app so you can read it.
  • Learning data: saved words, translations, highlights, bookmarks, reading position, practice results, streaks, XP and daily goals.
  • Settings: your English level, daily goal, translation language, reading theme and reminder preferences.
  • Translation cache: translations already looked up are saved so they work faster and offline next time.

If Android backup is turned on in your device settings, Android may include this app data in your personal Google backup, under your Google account's own privacy terms.

3. Google sign-in and the ad-free pass (optional)

You only need to sign in if you want to redeem a promo code for an ad-free pass. Sign-in uses Google Sign-In and Firebase Authentication, both provided by Google.

When you sign in, Google shares these details from your Google account with the app:

  • Your name, email address and profile picture
  • A unique user ID created by Firebase

When you redeem a code, we store the following in Google Cloud Firestore, linked to your user ID:

  • Your ad-free pass: the code you redeemed, its length, and when it started and ends
  • On the promo code itself: that it has been used, by which user ID, and when

We use this only to turn off ads for you on any device where you sign in. Your PDFs and learning data are never uploaded. You can sign out at any time in the app.

4. Advertising

PDFluent is free and shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner at the bottom of the Library screen and, at most once every 90 seconds, a full-screen ad after you finish a practice quiz. If you have an active ad-free pass, no ads are shown.

To show and measure ads, Google AdMob may collect:

  • Your device's advertising ID
  • Your IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device and app information, such as device model, Android version and app version
  • How you interact with ads, such as views and taps

Google may use this information to show you personalised ads. Google's use of this data is covered by Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Your choices: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in Android Settings → Google → Ads (the exact path depends on your device). You can also manage ad personalisation at My Ad Center.

6. Pronunciation

Pronunciation uses your device's built-in text-to-speech engine. The app sends the word to that engine on your device. The engine's provider, for example Google, handles it under its own privacy terms.

7. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app shows daily reminders and a Word of the Day. These are scheduled on your device and do not use any server. You can turn them off in the app's Profile settings or in Android settings.

8. Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to load ads, sign in and look up words online.
  • Advertising ID: used by Google AdMob to show and measure ads.
  • Notifications: for reminders and Word of the Day. This is optional.

The app does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone or files. You choose each PDF yourself through Android's file picker.

9. What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent your personal information.
  • We do not upload your PDFs, saved words, highlights or progress.
  • We do not use analytics or crash-reporting services.

10. How long we keep data

Data on your device stays until you delete it or uninstall the app. Your sign-in account and ad-free pass are kept in Firebase until you ask us to delete them. Data collected by Google AdMob is kept according to Google's own retention policies.

11. Deleting your data

  • Data on your device: remove saved words and books inside the app, or delete everything with Android Settings → Apps → PDFluent → Storage → Clear storage, or by uninstalling the app.
  • Your account and ad-free pass: email us at the address below from the Google account you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my PDFluent account". We will delete your Firebase account and ad-free pass within 30 days and confirm by email. Any remaining ad-free time is lost.
  • Advertising data: reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings, as described in section 4.

12. Children

PDFluent is a general education app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has signed in, contact us and we will delete their account.

13. Security

Data on your device is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. All connections to online services use encrypted HTTPS. Your ad-free pass in Firestore can only be read by your own signed-in account.

14. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above.
